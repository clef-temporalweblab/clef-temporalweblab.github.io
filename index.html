Why TemporalWebLab

The Web changes at different speeds.

Prices may change within hours, policies over months, and historical facts may remain stable for decades. Yet most information retrieval and question answering benchmarks evaluate only a fixed snapshot.

TemporalWebLab addresses two complementary questions: when should an answer be refreshed, and how can archived evidence establish what was supported at a particular time or how it changed?

Frequently asked questions

Start here.

Detailed participation rules will be added as the campaign resources are finalized.

Can we participate in only one task?+

Yes. Teams may participate in either Task 1 or Task 2 independently, or enter both.

How many runs can a team submit?+

Each team may submit up to three runs per task and designate one primary run for the official leaderboard.

Where will submissions be handled?+

Official submissions will be handled through TIRA to support secure, reproducible, and comparable evaluation.

When will data and baselines be available?+

The initial release is planned for October 2026. Exact release dates will be announced here.
