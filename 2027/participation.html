Participation

Teams may enter Task 1, Task 2, or both. This page will guide participants from registration through the final CLEF Working Notes submission.

How to participate

1. Register

Register for the CLEF 2027 evaluation campaign when the official form becomes available.

2. Choose tasks

Participate in either task independently or submit systems to both tasks.

3. Download resources

Use the released data, documentation, baselines, and validators to develop your system.

4. Submit runs

Upload official runs through TIRA and follow the final run-selection instructions.

Who can participate?

TemporalWebLab welcomes academic, industry, and independent research teams working on information retrieval, question answering, Web archives, temporal reasoning, RAG, and language models.

  • Teams may include participants from multiple institutions.
  • A team may participate in one or both tasks.
  • Each team should use a consistent team name across registration and submissions.

Runs

The current plan allows up to three runs per task. Teams should identify their primary run for official reporting and document the configuration of every submitted run.

Provisional: Final limits and designation rules will be confirmed in the official submission instructions.

System reporting

Participating teams will be invited to describe their methods and results in a CLEF Working Notes paper. Reports should explain preprocessing, retrieval or modeling choices, external resources, submitted run configurations, and reproducibility details.

Communication

Official campaign announcements, clarifications, and deadline changes will be posted on the Updates page. A participant communication channel and contact address will be linked here when available.
